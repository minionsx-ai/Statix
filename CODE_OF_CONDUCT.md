# Code of Conduct

## Purpose

Statix exists to make Rust musl builds with native dependencies more
repeatable. Project discussions should stay useful for maintainers,
contributors, and users trying to diagnose real build problems.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Keep technical disagreement focused on facts, logs, code, and reproducible
  evidence.
- Give enough context for others to understand your report or review comment.
- Accept maintainer decisions after a concern has been heard and answered.
- Help keep issue and pull request threads readable for future users.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Personal attacks, repeated bad-faith arguments, or deliberately derailing
  technical threads.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Spam, trolling, or unrelated promotion.
- Using security reports, private channels, or maintainer contact paths to
  pressure, harass, or bypass normal project process.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through GitHub.
Include relevant links, screenshots, or logs when they help establish what
happened.

Maintainers will handle reports with appropriate confidentiality, limit access
to people who need the information, and avoid retaliation against anyone who
reports a concern in good faith.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may respond with one or more of the following actions:

- a private reminder or warning
- editing or removing comments
- locking or hiding threads
- temporary restriction from project spaces
- permanent removal from project spaces
- escalation to the hosting platform when platform rules are involved

Enforcement should be proportionate to the behavior and its impact on the
project community.

## Scope

This code applies to Statix repositories, issues, pull requests,
discussions, and project-related communication channels. It does not replace
GitHub's terms, platform rules, or applicable law.
